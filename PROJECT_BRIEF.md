# Lumina Dental Studio — Project Brief

## 1. Project Overview

**Project Type:** Concept Project / Self-Initiated Freelance Portfolio Project  
**Client:** Lumina Dental Studio  
**Location:** Bonifacio Global City (BGC), Taguig, Metro Manila  
**Role:** Lead Product Designer & Frontend Developer  
**Timeline:** 2 Weeks  
**Deliverables:** Brand Strategy, Information Architecture, Visual Design System, and a Fully Responsive, Interactive HTML/CSS Homepage

## 2. Challenge & Brief

Lumina Dental Studio is a premium dental practice concept located in Bonifacio Global City. Founded by progressive clinicians, the practice aims to disrupt traditional, intimidating dental visits by offering a spa-like, tech-forward, and anxiety-free experience.

### Core Problem
Local dental websites can feel sterile, text-heavy, and difficult to navigate. Potential patients may experience friction when trying to understand high-end services, while clinics may rely too heavily on phone-based booking.

### Project Objectives
- Elevate the brand with a modern, calming interface that bridges medical trust and luxury wellness.
- Streamline information architecture and reduce cognitive load.
- Drive conversions through a persistent, friction-free appointment CTA.

## 3. Competitor & Market Research

The concept was benchmarked against two local clinics (Elevate Dental and Affinity Dental) and an international benchmark (Tend Dental).

### Key Takeaway
Local clinics tend to perform well at detailed procedural information and trust markers, while international benchmarks often excel at lifestyle branding and frictionless booking. Lumina combines local trust with a hospitality-driven digital experience.

## 4. Target Audience & Personas

### Busy Corporate Professional (28–45)
High-stress BGC corporate workers who value speed, efficiency, and aesthetics. They need fast navigation and low-friction booking.

### Anxious Aesthete (25–40)
Wellness-conscious patients seeking cosmetic enhancements who may experience dental anxiety. They need reassuring doctor information, warm visual cues, and transparent care details.

### Expat Executive (35–55)
Professionals relocated to Manila who expect international standards of care, polished communication, and clear information.

## 5. Information Architecture

The homepage is intentionally streamlined to reduce link bloat and guide visitors through a clear patient journey.

- **Home:** Hero, trust markers, services, Lumina experience, doctors, payment/HMO information, testimonials, FAQs, and final booking CTA.
- **Services:** Cosmetic Dentistry, Invisalign & Clear Aligners, and General & Preventive Care.
- **Our Doctors:** Clinical team profiles that establish human trust.
- **About:** The Lumina experience and care philosophy.
- **FAQs:** Practical questions that reduce uncertainty before booking.
- **Global CTA:** Persistent “Book an Appointment” action.

## 6. Visual Direction & Design System

**Aesthetic Direction:** Crisp, Clean & Tech-Forward (Option B). The visual language blends clinical hygiene with a calming, airy luxury-spa atmosphere.

### Color Palette
- Pure White `#FFFFFF`
- Light Gray `#F8F9FA`
- Dark Charcoal `#2B2D42`
- Soft Mint Green `#4CA794`

### Typography
- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Headings and prominent UI hierarchy.
- **Inter:** Body copy, supporting text, and utility content.

## 7. Technical Execution

The project is implemented as a responsive front-end experience using semantic HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

### Target Capabilities
- Responsive behavior from desktop through mobile.
- Accessible mobile navigation with ARIA states.
- Appointment interaction as a front-end portfolio prototype.
- Purposeful micro-interactions and transitions.
- Reduced-motion support and visible focus states.

## 8. Project Outcome

The intended result is a premium digital presence that transforms a traditionally intimidating dental interaction into a calm, human, and confidence-building experience. The concept balances conversion goals with patient empathy and clear information architecture.
